Before we finish, I have to tell you about Aurelia Bosch of Lisbon. Aurelia owns two hundred clocks. Two hundred of them, ticking in her house, and not a single one agrees with any other. A cacophony of mismatched time. And yet Aurelia has never once been late for anything in her entire life. She walks out her door, past the grandfather clock that says it is Tuesday when it is Friday, past the cuckoo that is eleven hours behind, and she arrives exactly on time.
